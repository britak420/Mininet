0. PURPOSE OF THIS REPORT
======================================================================

This report closes the open research/design question behind Mininet issue #21.

It is intentionally NOT another document saying:

  "personhood is hard,"
  "fund research,"
  "ask an expert,"
  "try TEE,"
  or
  "calibrate it later."

The research has been performed far enough here to make the launch decisions.

The report does four things:

  1. audits what the current repository actually implements;
  2. compares the design against established and current proof-of-personhood,
     Sybil-defense, anonymous-credential, ZK-membership, web-provenance and
     identity systems;
  3. selects one exact launch architecture with exact status thresholds,
     evidence caps, rate limits, privacy boundaries, bootstrap rules, voting
     credential semantics, human-share safety limits and launch metrics;
  4. gives engineering a deterministic conformance matrix.

After an external auditor of record adopts this report, no further generic
"research signal b / proof of personhood" study is a Gate #21 blocker.

Engineering evidence is still required. Evidence is not a second research
audit.

A future material redesign may reopen the affected scope, as Section 25
defines.


======================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE AUDIT OPINION
======================================================================

Mininet's core insight is correct:

  Whoever can manufacture accepted humans can manufacture votes and can claim
  the human-share.

Personhood is therefore the root security assumption for governance and
human-gated consensus.

The repository is also correct about one uncomfortable fact:

  there is no known 2026 construction that simultaneously provides:

    globally perfect biological uniqueness,
    no central issuer,
    no government dependency,
    no special biometric hardware,
    no vendor attestation root,
    full accessibility,
    weak-device operation,
    unlinkability,
    resistance to paid real-human collusion,
    coercion resistance,
    and zero false exclusions.

A security design that claims all of those has stopped being honest.

The correct Mininet objective is therefore NOT:

  "mathematically prove one biological human exists exactly once."

The approved objective is:

  "make an additional accepted human credential expensive, slow, socially
   scarce, privacy-preserving and continuously maintained; eliminate cheap
   software/device Sybils; prevent any one issuer/provider/wealth source from
   being sufficient; make one credential usable at most once per protected
   scope; and keep extractable value low enough that residual attacks are not
   economically attractive."

That is a defensible security target.

CURRENT-REPOSITORY FINDINGS

The current code is a useful prototype but is not the production design.

The most important findings are:

  * current `PromotionPolicy::whitepaper_default()` can reach
    `EvidenceQualifiedHuman` after only 30 days, two live signal sources and
    score 70;

  * current trust weights and policy values are caller-supplied/mutable rather
    than canonical consensus parameters;

  * `SignalSource::External(u32)` lets an arbitrary caller-defined external
    source enter the same generic score framework;

  * the current vouch graph stores raw persistent did:mini roots and edges;

  * the current `trust_scores()` function is described as "SybilRank-style"
    but is NOT the complete SybilRank algorithm because it returns raw
    propagated trust without SybilRank's final degree-normalization/ranking
    step;

  * the graph algorithm inherits the limited-attack-edge assumption that the
    literature itself identifies as fragile when Sybils purchase/cultivate
    real social edges;

  * the production behavioral/location entropy proof does not exist; the code
    correctly ships `NoEntropySource`;

  * current proximity evidence does not fully resist active relays/wormholes on
    ordinary software-timed bearers;

  * the specification requires the raw social/personhood graph not be exposed,
    while the current in-memory graph uses raw DIDs and the older on-chain
    score-root concept assumes recomputability from graph data;

  * the current economy default gives the human channel a one-year
    `human_vesting_ms`, which is incompatible with Mininet's frozen
    millennium-scale release and the documented <=0.1%-of-share first-year
    Sybil-security budget;

  * older SPEC-02 text proposes RLN secret revelation / personhood revocation
    and reverse-liability behavior that is contradicted by the later Deep Dive
    amendment forbidding de-personing for conduct and reverse liability.

AUDIT DECISION

Gate #21 is closed by selecting the following architecture.

1. RAW BEHAVIORAL/LOCATION ENTROPY IS REMOVED FROM THE LAUNCH CRITICAL PATH.

   It is not required at genesis.
   It has zero launch score weight.
   There is no TEE-attested "human classifier" requirement.

2. TEE / SECURE-ELEMENT ATTESTATION IS OPTIONAL DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH ONLY.

   It may strengthen local device-continuity confidence inside that class but
   may not add a mandatory score class, unlock MatureHuman by itself, or become
   a vendor root of trust.

3. THE CANONICAL RAW SOCIAL GRAPH IS REMOVED.

   Production Mininet does not publish or require a persistent personhood
   graph of DID-to-DID edges.

   Current `VouchGraph` and graph-analysis code becomes research/test tooling,
   not a consensus personhood dependency.

4. SOCIAL SCARCITY IS REPLACED BY RATE-LIMITED ANONYMOUS VOUCHING.

   Only a currently MatureHuman may issue a counted vouch.

   A MatureHuman may issue EXACTLY ONE counted vouch per 30-day personhood
   epoch.

   The vouch is a Semaphore-style anonymous membership signal over the
   previous finalized MatureHumanSet.

   The proof reveals a scope nullifier but NOT the voucher's DID or graph edge.

5. INITIAL MATURE-HUMAN PROMOTION REQUIRES:

   * >=365 days since first accepted evidence;
   * score >=75;
   * >=4 independent signal classes;
   * 24 valid mature-human vouches;
   * those vouches spanning >=10 distinct 30-day epochs;
   * at least 6 valid vouches inside one single 30-day epoch;
   * continuity evidence in >=9 of the preceding 12 personhood epochs;
   * either meaningful private-presence evidence OR meaningful private
     web/service continuity;
   * fresh evidence in the preceding 45 days.

6. THE "6 IN ONE EPOCH" RULE GUARANTEES BREADTH WITHOUT A PUBLIC SOCIAL GRAPH.

   Because every MatureHuman can emit only one counted vouch in that epoch,
   six accepted vouch proofs in that epoch require at least six distinct
   active MatureHuman membership secrets.

7. THE "24 VOUCHES" RULE CREATES A HARD SELF-REPLICATION BUDGET.

   A malicious mature identity has at most 12 vouch slots per 365 days.

   A new MatureHuman needs 24.

   Therefore, before considering the other signal requirements:

      attacker-created MatureHumans/year <= floor(A * 12 / 24)
                                          = floor(A / 2)

   where A is the number of already attacker-controlled MatureHuman
   credentials.

   An attacker with fewer than six MatureHuman credentials cannot satisfy the
   same-epoch breadth condition for even one self-contained candidate.

   Purchased honest humans remain a residual risk. The system does not pretend
   cryptography can stop a real human from deliberately selling cooperation.

8. PERSONHOOD STATUS IS SEPARATED FROM CONDUCT.

   Being offensive, criminal, unpopular, wrong, banned from a community, or a
   bad validator cannot make a human "not human."

   Operational sanctions apply to operational roles.

   Human status decays only from the holder's own lack of continuity evidence
   or from narrowly defined cryptographic duplicate/credential-invalidity
   facts.

   There is no jury empowered to strip baseline humanity.

9. THE PERSONHOOD SCORE IS CANONICAL, VERSIONED AND NOT CALLER-MUTABLE.

   `TrustWeights::set_weight()` and arbitrary policy injection are not allowed
   on the production consensus path.

   PersonhoodPolicyV1 is one deterministic protocol object.

10. EXTERNAL CREDENTIALS ARE OPTIONAL AND CAPPED.

    Passport/eID/Anon Aadhaar/World ID/Human Passport/etc. may contribute
    evidence, but:

      no provider is mandatory;
      no single provider contributes more than 5 points;
      the whole external-credential class contributes at most 10;
      no external credential bypasses vouch/time/diversity requirements.

11. WORLD ID / ORB IS NOT A MININET ROOT OF TRUST.

    It is technologically relevant evidence but depends on custom biometric
    enrollment hardware and a dedicated uniqueness infrastructure.

    That violates Mininet's no-indispensable-authority and weakest-device
    requirements if made mandatory.

12. HUMAN PASSPORT IS NOT A CONSENSUS SCORER.

    Its research demonstrates the usefulness of multi-signal scoring and
    empirical threshold calibration, but its hosted Models/Stamps APIs and
    mutable scoring weights are not acceptable as Mininet consensus truth.

13. PROOF OF HUMANITY IS NOT A MININET ROOT.

    Its public profile/photo/video/wallet linkage and challenge/arbitration
    posture conflict with Mininet structural privacy and no-de-personing.

14. IDENA-STYLE GLOBAL SYNCHRONOUS PUZZLES ARE NOT REQUIRED.

    They provide useful anti-parallelization ideas, but worldwide fixed-time
    ceremonies impose time-zone/accessibility/availability dependence and
    human-vs-AI puzzle assumptions that age poorly.

15. TLSNOTARY/WEB-PROVENANCE IS AN OPTIONAL LOW-CAPPED SIGNAL.

    It can privately prove narrow predicates about an existing web account or
    service interaction, but it inherits web PKI/provider/notary assumptions
    and must never become a required identity root.

16. SEMAPHORE IS USED FOR PRIVACY/RATE-LIMITING, NOT FOR PERSONHOOD ITSELF.

    Semaphore proves:
      membership in a set;
      one signal per scope.

    It does NOT decide whether the member is a human.

    Mininet personhood rules decide who enters the MatureHumanSet.

17. THE MATURE HUMAN SET IS CANONICAL CONSENSUS STATE DERIVED FROM VERIFIED
    PRIVACY-PRESERVING EVIDENCE EVENTS.

    It is NOT a committee opinion.

    Validators do not vote on who is human.
    No server returns "human=true."
    Full nodes deterministically verify and execute personhood evidence.

18. MAINNET REAL-VALUE GENESIS REQUIRES A MATURE PUBLIC TESTNET FIRST.

    Minimum:
      >=365 days of personhood history;
      >=2,048 active MatureHuman credentials;
      >=32 independently organized human cohorts;
      bootstrap cohort <=64;
      adversarial test criteria in Section 21;
      no raw graph/privacy leakage;
      first-year human-share extractability <=0.1% of lifetime allocation.

19. THE BOOTSTRAP TRUST ASSUMPTION IS EXPLICIT AND TEMPORARY.

    Exactly 64 BootstrapHumans initialize the testnet MatureHumanSet.

    They have:
      one vote each;
      one vouch slot per personhood epoch each;
      no extra trust weight;
      no permanent seed weight;
      no special mainnet role.

    Mainnet does not begin until the testnet set has expanded to >=2,048
    MatureHumans.

20. THE FIRST-YEAR HUMAN-SHARE SECURITY CEILING IS FROZEN AT 0.1%.

    No identity can make more than 10 basis points = 0.1% of its lifetime
    human-share allocation transferable during the first 365 days of its
    personhood maturation.

    Before MatureHuman status, provisional human-share accrual is not
    transferable.

21. GOVERNANCE AND VALIDATOR RIGHTS REQUIRE MATURE HUMAN STATUS.

    VouchedHuman and EvidenceQualifiedHuman do not vote and do not occupy a
    human validator slot.

22. ONE-HUMAN-ONE-ACTION USES SEPARATE NULLIFIER SCOPES.

    Vouching, voting, validator registration and human-share claiming use
    different scope domains so activity is unlinkable across functions.

23. NO HUMAN CAN BE REQUIRED TO PROVIDE:

    biometrics,
    government ID,
    bank account,
    paid service,
    special hardware,
    mobile carrier identity,
    legal name,
    exact location,
    contact graph,
    browsing history.

    A non-government, non-biometric, non-bank, non-TEE route to MatureHuman is
    mandatory and is specified in this report.

FINAL GATE #21 AUDIT DISPOSITION

  PERSONHOOD RESEARCH / DESIGN: PASS / COMPLETE

  CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION: PARTIAL

  PERFECT BIOLOGICAL UNIQUENESS: NOT CLAIMED

  APPROVED SECURITY CLAIM:
    cheap automated/device Sybils are structurally blocked;
    accepted credentials require scarce mature-human sponsorship, long-lived
    multi-class evidence and time;
    one accepted credential acts once per protected scope;
    patient paid-human collusion remains a named residual.

2. SOURCE-DERIVED CURRENT STATE
======================================================================

This section describes current Mininet, not the approved replacement.

2.1 Issue #21

Issue #21 correctly says the original behavioral/location entropy idea is
unsolved research and asks for a survey of private, weak-device-compatible
human continuity mechanisms.

That research question is answered by this report.

2.2 D-0075 / current Human Continuity design

The current design document already made a good conceptual move:

  away from:
    "prove a raw behavioral/location trace looks human"

  toward:
    "accumulate independent private human-existence evidence over time."

It proposes:
  private `human_secret`;
  pairwise pseudonyms;
  local evidence stamps;
  no raw trace;
  multiple trust domains;
  time-separated evidence;
  optional government/web/device/liveness classes;
  no single signal sufficient.

That direction is retained.

2.3 Current status implementation is materially weaker than that design

Current production-shaped defaults in `mini-uniqueness::status` are:

  Vouched score threshold:             30
  EvidenceQualified score threshold:   70
  minimum full age:                    30 days
  minimum distinct live sources:       2
  required source:                     VouchingGraph

Trust weights:
  PhysicalPresence:                    100
  VouchingGraph:                        70
  BehavioralEntropy:                    60
  arbitrary External default:           20

The score denominator includes only sources with present evidence.

This means a small number of signal classes can saturate the normalized score.

The code itself acknowledges that it cannot prove unique humans and deliberately
uses the name `EvidenceQualifiedHuman`.

That naming discipline is correct and is retained.

2.4 Current policy is caller-controlled

`TrustWeights::set_weight()` can alter a source weight.

`PromotionPolicy` is supplied by the caller.

`SignalSource::External(u32)` can name arbitrary external sources.

That is acceptable in a library prototype.

It is unacceptable for deterministic consensus personhood.

Two honest nodes must not be able to use different personhood weights or
thresholds because a caller/config file supplied different values.

2.5 Current VouchGraph exposes the exact privacy structure the Deep Dive says
must not be published

`VouchGraph` stores:

  HashMap<Did, HashSet<Did>>

Edges are real identity-root relations.

That is a raw social graph.

The Deep Dive identifies the personhood graph as the highest deanonymization
risk and explicitly requires that raw edges never be public.

Therefore current VouchGraph cannot be the canonical public personhood state.

2.6 Current graph function is not complete SybilRank

Current `trust_scores()`:

  * initializes trust on seeds;
  * propagates mass for a bounded number of iterations;
  * divides each node's outgoing mass by degree;
  * returns the resulting raw trust mass.

The original SybilRank algorithm additionally ranks by DEGREE-NORMALIZED FINAL
TRUST:

  T_hat(v) = T_w(v) / degree(v)

because the final normalization removes node-degree bias and is part of its
security argument.

Current Mininet does not perform that final step.

Therefore the phrase "SybilRank-style" is acceptable.
"SybilRank security" is not.

2.7 Even complete SybilRank would not be a sufficient root

The original paper explicitly relies on relatively few attack edges between
honest and Sybil regions.

Later evaluations show graph defenses degrade when Sybils acquire more genuine
social links.

That is exactly the attack expected when:
  real humans sell vouches;
  fraud farms recruit humans;
  social accounts are purchased;
  nation-state actors cultivate long-term identities.

Therefore graph rank can be useful research telemetry but must not be the
single canonical human decision.

2.8 Behavioral entropy is correctly absent

`NoEntropySource` is the only actual implementation.

That is the correct behavior today.

The production design must keep "absent" safer than silently inventing a weak
heuristic.

2.9 Current presence is evidence of devices, not proof of unique humans

`mini-presence` correctly says this itself.

It verifies:
  delegated devices;
  signatures;
  channel binding;
  fresh nonces;
  proximity transport;
  timing/range policy.

But:
  two colluding roots can create evidence;
  software timing cannot fully defeat active relay/wormhole;
  identity-root != unique human.

Presence is valuable evidence.
It is not a personhood oracle.

2.10 Current one-year human vesting is inconsistent with the frozen security
model

`IssuancePolicy::d0074()` currently sets:

  human_vesting_ms = YEAR_MS

That creates a one-year envelope.

SPEC-05 and the integrated design instead say:

  human share releases over a millennium-scale horizon;
  early vesting is deliberately tiny;
  first-year extraction should be <= approximately 0.1% of the share.

A one-year vesting envelope cannot remain the production human-share rule.

This is a personhood-security finding because the value extractable before
Sybil detection is part of the Sybil attack economics.

2.11 Older RLN/de-personing language is superseded

Older SPEC-02 text says RLN quota violation can reveal a secret and cause
personhood revocation / score punishment.

The later Deep Dive explicitly removes:
  reverse liability;
  conduct-based personhood penalties;
  accusation-driven de-personing.

This report follows the later rule.

RLN-style secret revelation is NOT used for baseline personhood in V1.

3. WHAT THE EXTERNAL RESEARCH ACTUALLY SHOWS
======================================================================

3.1 SybilRank

Useful result:
social-graph structure can make cheap dense Sybil farms rank poorly when the
honest/Sybil cut has few attack edges.

Important original details:
  early-terminated random walk;
  O(log n) iterations;
  degree-normalized final trust;
  trusted seeds;
  limited attack edges.

Important limitation:
the original paper itself acknowledges highly connected Sybils can rank well.
Subsequent studies report serious degradation when attack edges are not scarce.

Mininet conclusion:
  social scarcity is useful;
  a public graph scorer is not the production root.

3.2 Semaphore

Semaphore V4 provides:
  anonymous Merkle-group membership;
  anonymous message signaling;
  scope/nullifier protection against repeated signaling in the same scope.

Its docs are explicit that applications decide group membership.

Semaphore does not prove humanity.

The V4 trusted setup ceremony reportedly involved more than 400 participants in
July 2024.

Mininet conclusion:
  APPROVE Semaphore-style membership/nullifier proofs for:
    voting,
    vouch-slot use,
    validator uniqueness,
    human-share claims.

  REJECT the statement:
    "Semaphore solves personhood."

3.3 Rate Limiting Nullifier (RLN)

RLN is designed to rate-limit anonymous actors and can expose secret material
when a rate is exceeded.

That can be appropriate for anonymous spam systems.

It is dangerous as a baseline human credential primitive where revealing an
identity secret can become:
  unmasking;
  coercion;
  an indirect de-personing mechanism.

Mininet conclusion:
  RLN is not needed for PersonhoodPolicyV1.
  Standard one-signal-per-scope Semaphore nullifiers provide the exact vouch
  quota needed with less complexity.

Future non-personhood anonymous spam actions may use RLN separately.

3.4 W3C Verifiable Credentials + BBS

Current BBS Data Integrity work provides:
  selective disclosure;
  unlinkable derived proofs;
  anonymous holder binding;
  scoped credential pseudonyms.

This is valuable for optional external evidence.

Mininet conclusion:
  APPROVE BBS/VC-style evidence interfaces as optional adapters;
  do not create one mandatory credential issuer.

3.5 World ID

World's current system demonstrates that:
  custom iris hardware;
  biometric liveness;
  private uniqueness comparison;
  MPC;
  Semaphore/ZK presentation

can create a strong practical PoH system.

It also demonstrates the tradeoff:
  an Orb or equivalent enrollment route is a special hardware/infrastructure
  dependency.

World's own published architecture has custom hardware and a dedicated
uniqueness infrastructure.

Mininet conclusion:
  useful OPTIONAL evidence;
  max one external-provider contribution;
  never mandatory;
  never sufficient;
  no Orb operator becomes Mininet's personhood issuer.

3.6 Human Passport

Human Passport demonstrates:
  multi-signal "stamps";
  weighted scoring;
  empirical threshold calibration;
  the unavoidable false-positive / false-negative tradeoff.

Its 2026 docs say:
  a default Unique Humanity threshold is 20;
  higher thresholds improve Sybil rejection while increasing honest-user
  exclusion;
  scoring weights are updated as attacker behavior changes.

Its hosted Models API requires an API key and returns centrally computed model
scores.

Mininet conclusion:
  adopt the lesson:
    multi-signal systems need measured calibration.

  reject:
    hosted Human Passport scorer as consensus truth.

  optional individually verifiable credentials may contribute within external
  caps.

3.7 Proof of Humanity

Proof of Humanity historically uses:
  public profiles;
  wallet linkage;
  photos/video;
  human vouching;
  public challenge/arbitration;
  deposits.

That can produce useful uniqueness evidence.

It does not fit Mininet's privacy model.

Mininet conclusion:
  not a mandatory/root method.

3.8 Idena

Idena's synchronous ceremony attacks the multi-account problem by requiring
humans to solve time-bounded puzzles at the same global period.

Strength:
  anti-parallelization.

Costs:
  fixed global timing;
  availability/time-zone/accessibility burden;
  puzzle assumptions change as AI improves;
  identity-account transfer remains technically possible.

Mininet conclusion:
  do not require global puzzle ceremonies.
  Keep synchronous live interaction as a small optional/local signal.

3.9 TLSNotary

Current TLSNotary lets a user prove selected facts from a real TLS session via
MPC and selective disclosure.

The notary/verifier need not see application plaintext in MPC mode.

But:
  an online verifier/notary participates;
  web PKI is trusted for server identity;
  web provider account policies remain external;
  proof generation has nontrivial resource/network cost.

Mininet conclusion:
  suitable for OPTIONAL web-life continuity;
  max 15 points total;
  no provider/notary mandatory;
  no usernames, browsing history or content retained.

3.10 Government/eID ZK systems

Anon Aadhaar demonstrates:
  proving possession of government-signed identity data inside ZK;
  selective disclosure;
  nullifiers;
  no need to reveal the Aadhaar number itself.

Similar e-passport systems demonstrate the same broad pattern.

Strength:
  high cost to mass-fabricate.

Limit:
  government issuer is the root;
  coverage is unequal;
  documents can be unavailable, discriminatory, revoked or coercively linked.

Mininet conclusion:
  optional external evidence only;
  max 5 points per issuer family;
  never mandatory.

3.11 No single existing project satisfies the Mininet constitution

World ID:
  strong uniqueness,
  special biometric hardware/infrastructure.

Proof of Humanity:
  human review,
  weak privacy/public linkage.

Human Passport:
  broad multi-signal,
  mutable hosted scoring/API dependencies.

Idena:
  no biometrics,
  synchronous ceremony/accessibility burden.

Government ZK:
  strong issuer authenticity,
  government root.

BrightID/social graphs:
  no required biometrics,
  graph assumptions and social-edge market risk.

Therefore Mininet should combine:
  anonymous mature-human scarcity,
  time,
  multiple private evidence classes,
  low early extraction,
  deterministic consensus,
  and explicit residual risk.

That is the design below.

4. SECURITY CLAIM — EXACTLY WHAT MININET MAY AND MAY NOT SAY
======================================================================

APPROVED PUBLIC CLAIM

"Mininet uses a privacy-preserving, continuously re-earned personhood system
designed to make additional governance/human-share identities slow and costly
to create. Mature status requires long-lived evidence across multiple
independent classes and scarce anonymous vouches from existing mature humans.
No government, biometric vendor, company, or single verifier decides who is
human. Protected actions use anonymous membership proofs and per-scope
nullifiers so one accepted credential acts once in that scope."

APPROVED HONEST LIMIT

"Mininet does not claim perfect biological uniqueness. A patient, well-funded
attacker who recruits or pays real humans and sustains convincing evidence over
long periods can still penetrate the system. The protocol limits the speed,
cost and extractable value of that attack rather than pretending it is
mathematically impossible."

PROHIBITED CLAIMS

  "one account is cryptographically guaranteed to equal one biological human"

  "Sybil-proof"

  "impossible to fake"

  "perfect proof of personhood"

  "six vouches prove six legal identities"

  "presence proves a human"

  "government ID proves global uniqueness"

  "World ID proves Mininet uniqueness"

  "Semaphore proves humanity"

  "the social graph catches all Sybils"

  "vote credentials are impossible to sell"

No digital protocol can stop a willing human from:
  sharing a secret,
  selling cooperation,
  acting under coercion.

The protocol is designed to make such attacks human-scale rather than
machine-scale.

5. CANONICAL PERSONHOOD TERMS
======================================================================

`did:mini HumanRoot`
  Self-sovereign identity root.
  It solves continuity/control of an identity, not unique-human overcounting.

`PersonhoodSecret`
  High-entropy private secret used to derive the anonymous personhood
  membership identity.

`PersonhoodCommitment`
  Public commitment inserted into personhood state/Merkle sets.

`PersonhoodApplication`
  Temporary, scoped application handle used while accumulating evidence.

`EvidenceEvent`
  Canonical proof-bearing event that contributes to one application without
  exposing raw private data.

`VouchedHuman`
  Provisional human-like status.
  No governance vote.
  No human validator slot.
  No transferable human-share entitlement.

`EvidenceQualifiedHuman`
  Stronger 180-day intermediate state.
  May accumulate provisional locked human-share entitlement.
  No governance vote.
  No human validator slot.

`MatureHuman`
  365-day status that may:
    vote once per decision;
    satisfy the human side of validator eligibility;
    claim the transferable portion of the human-share under the slow schedule;
    issue one anonymous counted vouch per vouch epoch.

`DormantHuman`
  Previously MatureHuman whose own continuity evidence fell below maintenance
  requirements.
  Historical humanity is not erased.
  Existing value is not seized.
  Voting/new human-share accrual/validator eligibility pauses until re-earned.

`BootstrapHuman`
  One of the exactly 64 initial TESTNET mature credentials.
  Has no extra vote/weight/vouch capacity.
  Exists solely to break the zero-member bootstrap cycle.

`MatureHumanSet`
  Canonical privacy-preserving group of active MatureHuman commitments.

`ScopeNullifier`
  ZK-derived value proving one group member has used one protected scope.

6. PERSONHOOD POLICY V1 — FROZEN LAUNCH DEFAULT
======================================================================

PersonhoodPolicyV1 is a canonical protocol object.

No runtime node config can override it.

Time source:
  finalized canonical chain time / personhood epoch.

Do NOT trust:
  device wall clock
  HTTP server clock
  external timestamp authority.

PERSONHOOD_EPOCH:
  30 canonical days.

PRESENCE_PERIOD:
  90 canonical days.

INITIAL MATURE AGE:
  365 canonical days.

INTERMEDIATE QUALIFIED AGE:
  180 canonical days.

MATURE REEVALUATION:
  every personhood epoch.

Status maintenance grace:
  30 days after a status would otherwise become Dormant, to tolerate temporary
  connectivity/device problems.

During grace:
  existing vote eligibility remains only until current already-open decision
  scopes close;
  no new validator epoch registration;
  no new human-share accrual after grace begins.

No governance vote can shorten the initial 365-day MatureHuman age below the
V1 floor.

No governance vote can make:
  biometric,

mandatory.

Score is:

MAX: 30
REQUIRED FOR QUALIFIED AND MATURE

7.2 PRIVATE PHYSICAL-PRESENCE CONTINUITY
MAX: 20
OPTIONAL
----------------------------------------------------------------------

10 points:
  at least 2 accepted presence endorsements from 2 distinct MatureHuman
  membership proofs inside one 90-day presence period.

20 points:
  the above condition is satisfied in at least two different presence periods
  separated by >=90 days.

  network
  applicant
  presence period.

This makes two accepted endorsements in one period require two distinct mature

Presence is still vulnerable to colluding humans and some relay scenarios.
That is why it is worth at most 20 and never sufficient by itself.

9. ANONYMOUS VOUCH PROTOCOL V1
======================================================================

9.1 Group

Use the previous finalized personhood epoch's:

  MatureHumanSetRoot

This prevents same-epoch recursive admission/vouch amplification.

9.2 Epoch scope

For vouch epoch e:

  vouch_scope =
    H(
      "mininet/personhood/vouch-scope/v1",
      network_id,
      e
    )

The scope is identical for every candidate in that epoch.

Semaphore-style semantics therefore permit one accepted signal from one mature
membership identity for the entire vouch epoch.

That is the scarce resource.

9.3 Applicant object

PersonhoodApplicationV1:

  version
  network_id
  application_id
  applicant_personhood_commitment
  application_public_key
  opened_personhood_epoch
  policy_version
  expiry_epoch

`application_id`:

  H(
    "mininet/personhood/application/v1",
    canonical application object excluding application_id
  )

Application public key:
  fresh application-scoped key;
  does not need to be a day-to-day DID.

9.4 Vouch signal

  vouch_signal =
    H(
      "mininet/personhood/vouch-signal/v1",
      network_id,
      application_id,
      applicant_personhood_commitment,
      policy_version,
      expiry_epoch
    )

9.5 Proof

AnonymousVouchV1 contains:

  version
  network_id
  personhood_epoch
  mature_set_root
  application_id
  vouch_signal
  nullifier
  semaphore_proof

Verifier requires:

  root is the canonical prior-epoch MatureHumanSetRoot;
  proof verifies membership;
  proof signal exactly equals canonical vouch_signal;
  scope exactly equals canonical vouch_scope;
  nullifier unused in this vouch epoch;
  application active/not expired.

9.6 Privacy property

Public network learns:

  a member of prior MatureHumanSet
  spent their one vouch opportunity this epoch
  on this application.

Public network does NOT learn:

  voucher DID
  voucher human-root
  voucher's other pseudonyms
  their previous/future vouch nullifiers across epochs
  their physical location.

9.7 Anti-farm bound

Each MatureHuman:
  12 slots/year.

Initial MatureHuman:
  24 vouches.

Thus pure self-sponsored expansion from A malicious mature credentials is
bounded by A/2 new credentials/year BEFORE:

  time;
  non-vouch class;
  continuity;
  score;
  same-epoch six-person breadth.

The same-epoch condition additionally requires:
  A >=6
for a closed attacker group to make even one candidate satisfy Mature
breadth.

9.8 No paid-vouch fantasy

A genuine human may sell their one monthly slot.

This cannot be cryptographically prevented without:
  surveillance,
  coercion,
  identity disclosure,
  or an external authority.

Mininet mitigates it by:
  scarcity;
  maturation time;
  multi-class evidence;
  low first-year value;
  broad genesis;
  no reverse-liability punishment that creates authoritarian leverage.

Paid real-human collusion is residual risk, not a solved claim.

10. PRIVATE PRESENCE ENDORSEMENT V1
======================================================================

10.1 Local session

Candidate and MatureHuman witness run existing mini-presence verification.

The local verification must check:
  delegated keys;
  signatures;
  KEL state;
  fresh nonces;
  channel binding;
  proximity bearer;
  RTT policy;
  optional UWB when available.

10.2 Raw evidence stays local

Compute:

  presence_digest =
    H(
      "mininet/personhood/presence-local-digest/v1",
      canonical verified local PresenceVerdict/transcript
    )

The raw transcript may be retained encrypted by the candidate according to
local policy for a bounded time.

It is not consensus data.

10.3 Anonymous witness proof

For presence period p and application A:

  presence_scope =
    H(
      "mininet/personhood/presence-scope/v1",
      network_id,
      application_id,
      p
    )

Witness produces MatureHumanSet membership proof over:

  presence_signal =
    H(
      "mininet/personhood/presence-signal/v1",
      application_id,
      presence_digest,
      p
    )

Because the same scope is used per witness/applicant/period, one witness cannot
count twice for that applicant in one period.

Two distinct accepted nullifiers in the same period therefore require two
distinct mature membership identities.

10.4 Honest limitation

The chain verifies:
  a MatureHuman anonymously endorsed the digest.

It does NOT independently measure the physical distance.

A malicious mature witness can lie/collude.

Presence weight and multi-signal requirements explicitly account for that.

10.5 UWB

If UWB exists:
  use it as additional local anti-relay evidence.

If it does not:
  user remains eligible through other paths.

No UWB vendor becomes a personhood authority.

======================================================================
15. WHY THE RAW SOCIAL GRAPH IS REMOVED
======================================================================

This is a deliberate deviation from older SPEC-02 mechanics while preserving
their security intent.

OLD PURPOSE OF GRAPH:
  make fake internal edges cheap/irrelevant;
  require connection to real humans;
  prevent isolated fake clusters from self-certifying.

NEW MECHANISM:

  1. only an existing MatureHuman can vouch;

  2. vouch is anonymously membership-proven;

  3. one MatureHuman has one vouch slot per 30 days;

  4. new MatureHuman needs 24 vouches over one year;

  5. new MatureHuman needs six distinct mature membership identities in one
     month;

  6. candidate needs multiple additional continuity classes.

This preserves "honest-edge scarcity" without publishing a graph.

Benefits:

  * no global DID adjacency list;
  * no seed-weight algorithm;
  * no permanent founder trust score;
  * no privacy-preserving global graph-computation problem;
  * no SybilRank limited-attack-edge assumption as consensus truth;
  * deterministic local verification;
  * simple quantitative sponsorship budget.

Tradeoff:

  graph algorithms can sometimes identify suspicious cluster structure that the
  anonymous-vouch mechanism cannot see.

Accepted solution:
  keep graph analysis only in consented/synthetic test telemetry and external
  research, not as the authority deciding baseline humanity.

This is a privacy-first simplification.

16. BOOTSTRAP / COLD START
======================================================================

A zero-member MatureHumanSet cannot issue the first vouch.

A bootstrap trust assumption is unavoidable.

Mininet must name it instead of hiding it.

16.1 Testnet bootstrap cohort

Exactly:
  64 BootstrapHumans.

Why 64:
  inside the project's previously recommended 30–100 seed range;
  large enough to avoid a tiny founder clique;
  small enough for a serious human verification ceremony;
  power-of-two convenience is secondary, not security.

16.2 Diversity requirements

The 64-person testnet bootstrap must satisfy:

  >=12 independently organized social/physical cohorts;

  no cohort >8 members;

  >=8 countries/territories represented;

  >=3 continents represented;

  no one country/territory >16 members;

  no one employer/company/foundation/funder controls >4 members;

  no one individual controls >1 member;

  no shared credential secrets/devices.

Private exact location/legal identity need not be published.

Public bootstrap record contains:
  64 personhood commitments;
  aggregate diversity counts;
  signed one-slot declarations;
  ceremony transcript hash;
  auditor-of-record attestation.

16.3 Bootstrap verification

Every BootstrapHuman must:

  appear in a synchronous live ceremony;
  complete at least two co-presence verifications where practical;
  be vouched for by at least four other bootstrap participants;
  sign a one-person/one-bootstrap-slot declaration;
  control their own personhood secret;
  use a distinct did:mini root/device environment.

At least one of each person's vouches must come from outside their immediate
local cohort.

This does not mathematically prove uniqueness.

It is the explicit genesis trust assumption.

16.4 No special power

BootstrapHuman gets:
  exactly one MatureHuman membership credential.

No:
  extra vote;
  extra vouch capacity;
  permanent seed score;
  veto;
  admin role;
  guaranteed validator slot.

From first normal personhood epoch:
  same rules as any MatureHuman.

16.5 Testnet maturation before mainnet

The 64 bootstrap credentials initialize public TESTNET only.

Real-value MAINNET may not rely on a 64-person personhood population.

Mainnet exit criterion:
  >=2,048 active MatureHuman credentials.

At 2,048:
  original bootstrap cohort is at most 3.125% of the active mature set.

No "founder weight" exists, so dilution is actual membership dilution rather
than an adjustable graph coefficient.

16.6 Mainnet genesis import

Eligible testnet MatureHumans can migrate to mainnet using a privacy-preserving
migration proof:

  prove membership in the final accepted testnet MatureHumanSet snapshot;
  derive a new mainnet personhood commitment;
  consume a one-time migration nullifier.

The mainnet leaf need not equal the testnet leaf.

Migration scope:
  H(
    "mininet/personhood/mainnet-migration/v1",
    testnet_network_id,
    mainnet_network_id,
    final_testnet_root
  )

Each testnet mature credential migrates at most once.

This prevents simple testnet/mainnet personhood-linkability.

No testnet MINI value needs to migrate.

17. HUMAN-SHARE SAFETY ENVELOPE
======================================================================

Personhood security and vesting are coupled.

Current one-year `human_vesting_ms` is rejected for production.

17.1 Frozen first-year ceiling

For one person's full lifetime human-share allocation S:

  maximum TRANSFERABLE by 365 days of personhood maturation:

    0.001 * S

which is:
  0.1%
  10 basis points.

17.2 Before MatureHuman

VouchedHuman:
  zero transferable human share.

EvidenceQualifiedHuman:
  may accrue provisional entitlement;
  zero transferable human share.

17.3 Personhood maturity multiplier

For the first year's security envelope, preserve the D-0075 maturity idea as a
multiplier on the tiny year-one tranche, not as a percentage of the entire
lifetime share.

Year-one tranche maximum:
  10 bps of lifetime share.

Maturity multiplier reference points:

  day   0:  10%
  day  30:  25%
  day  90:  50%
  day 180:  75%
  day 365: 100%

Thus the maximum provisional cumulative share-equivalent at the points is:

  day   0: 1.0 bp  = 0.010% lifetime share
  day  30: 2.5 bp  = 0.025%
  day  90: 5.0 bp  = 0.050%
  day 180: 7.5 bp  = 0.075%
  day 365:10.0 bp  = 0.100%

But transferability remains ZERO until MatureHuman.

At initial maturity:
  at most 10 bps becomes transferable.

17.4 Later millennium curve

Gate #47/#50 economic calibration may choose the exact centuries-scale release
after year one.

It may NOT violate:
  frozen millennium-horizon floor;
  first-year <=0.1%;
  personhood-conditioned accrual;
  no money acceleration.

17.5 Why this matters

If a fake candidate is detected/fails before maturity:
  it extracts zero human-share liquidity.

Even a fake that reaches initial MatureHuman has only <=0.1% of its lifetime
allocation liquid after the entire first year.

This sharply reduces incentive for short-lived farms.

======================================================================
18. GOVERNANCE, VALIDATOR AND VOUCH NULLIFIERS
======================================================================

18.1 Governance

Only Active MatureHuman at proposal snapshot may vote.

Proposal stores:
  mature_set_root_at_open.

Vote proof scope:
  network_id + proposal_id.

One nullifier:
  one accepted action per mature membership.

Vote weight:
  exactly 1.

No:
  stake
  MINI balance
  contribution
  rank
  founder status
  storage
changes weight.

18.2 Validator

Human eligibility proof uses:

  mature_set_root_at_consensus_epoch

scope:
  network + consensus_epoch.

One mature personhood secret:
  at most one active human validator registration per epoch.

Storage/availability requirements can affect whether the validator is eligible
or selected.

They do not change BFT vote weight above one.

18.3 Vouch

Scope:
  one global vouch scope per 30-day personhood epoch.

One MatureHuman:
  one counted vouch that epoch.

No rank/tier buys more capacity in V1.

This intentionally rejects older "verification capacity ladder" designs that
would let early or high-rank people multiply personhood-creation power.

18.4 Human share

One share claim per release scope per mature membership.

The claim proof is unlinkable from governance/vouch/validator scopes.

18.5 No delegation

A protocol-level vote credential cannot create a delegable voting token.

A user can always reveal/share their own secret voluntarily.
No software can make voluntary secret transfer physically impossible.

Mininet must not market "unsellable vote" as an absolute theorem.

19. NO DE-PERSONING / REVOCATION MODEL
======================================================================

19.1 Baseline rule

Personhood rating describes evidence of humanity only.

It is not:
  reputation
  trustworthiness
  morality
  legal status
  moderation standing.

19.2 Conduct

The following NEVER lowers humanness score:

  disliked speech
  community ban
  content blacklist
  political position
  accusation
  criminal allegation
  validator equivocation
  bad code review
  failure to follow community norms.

19.3 Operational consequences

A validator who equivocates:
  loses/cools down validator privilege according to consensus rules.

A spammer:
  can be filtered/rate-limited at reach/application layers.

A malicious maintainer:
  can lose maintainership.

A real human remains a real human.

19.4 Dormancy

Own inactivity can pause active eligibility because the constitution requires
continuously re-earned status.

Dormancy is automatic/deterministic.

No human adjudicator.

19.5 Duplicate personhood

A duplicate credential can be retired only on:

  exact cryptographic duplicate commitment/nullifier contradiction;
  holder-authorized merge/rotation;
  future positive cryptographic proof type explicitly included in a reviewed
  PersonhoodPolicy version.

A social accusation is not proof.

An optional external credential collision alone does not automatically
de-person because an issuer can make mistakes.

19.6 No reverse liability

If Alice vouched for Bob and Bob later becomes suspicious:

  Alice does not lose humanness;
  Alice does not lose vote;
  Alice does not lose owned share.

Alice already paid the scarce cost:
  that epoch's vouch opportunity.

That is sufficient protocol-level skin in the game without creating a
retaliation weapon.

27. OVERALL JUDGMENT
======================================================================

DOES THE APPROVED PERSONHOOD DESIGN SERVE A FREE INTERNET FOR HUMANITY?

YES.

The current prototype does not yet prove that outcome.

The approved architecture does, under an honest and explicit threat model.

Why YES:

  * one government does not decide humanity;
  * one company does not decide humanity;
  * one biometric vendor does not decide humanity;
  * one founder does not decide humanity;
  * one social graph operator does not decide humanity;
  * one AI classifier does not decide humanity;
  * money cannot buy a faster protocol path to human status;
  * raw social edges are not canonical/public;
  * raw location/biometrics/web history are not required;
  * mature sponsorship is scarce and anonymous;
  * status takes real time;
  * multiple independent evidence classes are required;
  * a self-contained attacker with fewer than six mature credentials cannot
    bootstrap another MatureHuman;
  * an attacker with A mature credentials has at most floor(A/2)
    self-sponsored new mature credentials per year before other gates;
  * early human-share extraction is <=0.1% after a full year;
  * only MatureHuman gets one vote;
  * no conduct authority can declare a dissident "not human";
  * optional external identity systems remain replaceable edges;
  * the bootstrap cohort has no permanent trust multiplier and is diluted to
    <=3.125% before real-value mainnet.

The design does NOT eliminate a patient adversary who pays or coerces genuine
humans.

That is the irreducible residual.

The correct success standard is not:
  "no adversary can ever create another credential."

The correct standard is:
  "machine-scale forgery becomes human-scale infiltration, and human-scale
   infiltration is rate-limited, time-limited, economically bounded and unable
   to buy extra vote weight."
