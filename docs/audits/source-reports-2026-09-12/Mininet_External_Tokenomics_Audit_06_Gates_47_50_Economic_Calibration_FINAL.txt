======================================================================
0. AUDIT QUESTION
======================================================================

The question is not:

  "Can MINI have a token?"

The question is:

  "Can Mininet create transferable value without allowing wealth, early arrival,
   infrastructure scale, exchange access, treasury funding, oracle influence, or
   market manipulation to become a path to political control or a permanent
   protocol-created aristocracy?"

The answer after the decisions in this report is:

  YES.

The current implementation does not yet satisfy the answer.

The most important current defects are not speculative. They are visible in the
reviewed code:

  F6-01 — `IssuancePolicy::d0074()` gives Human Share grants a 365-day vesting
           period. That conflicts with the frozen millennium-horizon intent and
           the later quantitative decision that the first year should release
           no more than approximately 0.1% of a Human Share.

  F6-02 — per-epoch issuance is prorated from each call's
           `opening_circulating`. There is no year-to-date budget ledger. If a
           caller feeds the newly enlarged supply into each monthly epoch, a
           nominal 3% annual ceiling compounds to approximately 3.041596% over
           twelve equal monthly periods. Daily rebasing gives approximately
           3.045326%. A hard 3% annual ceiling must not depend on caller
           discipline.

  F6-03 — `IssuancePolicy` is caller-supplied. Validation checks that channel
           ppm values add to no more than the total and that vesting is nonzero,
           but does not make 2% Human Share / 0.75% service / 0.25% contribution
           the canonical production envelope. A locally constructed policy can
           radically change distribution while remaining structurally valid.

  F6-04 — `mini-econ-sim` is only a first harness. It models no prices, market
           depth, fee burden, reserve assets, oracle attacks, vesting liquidity,
           service concavity, contribution timing, source correlation, or
           validator/economic interaction. It also assigns one half of service
           issuance to the whale by construction rather than deriving capture
           from a reward mechanism.

  F6-05 — current `mini-reward::accrue_storage` computes raw reward linearly from
           committed GiB and then applies repeat decay/window caps. This is not
           the frozen `x^a`, 0<a<1 concave resource curve promised by the
           economic design.

  F6-06 — `mini-treasury::RateHistory` records arbitrary governed scalar rates.
           It has no multi-source price provenance, source-domain independence,
           time-window commitment, manipulation halt, reserve haircut, or
           contribution-epoch binding.

  F6-07 — `mini-treasury::rate::mint_amount_micro` multiplies in `u128` but then
           casts the quotient to `u64` with `as u64`. A quotient above
           `u64::MAX` is not rejected as monetary overflow. Monetary arithmetic
           must fail closed.

  F6-08 — the current contribution receipt is bookkeeping, not proof of receipt.
           It still names Bitcoin/Monero and `NoExternalReceiptOracle` always
           returns false. Contribution minting is therefore correctly not
           production-capable, but the economic layer must explicitly refuse
           issuance whenever a chain-specific receipt verifier is unavailable.

  F6-09 — D-0073's 50% XRP / 40% XMR / 10% contingency reserve target has an
           asymmetric worst single-asset -80% shock: -40% reserve value if XRP
           suffers the shock versus -32% if XMR does.

  F6-10 — D-0073 permits the contribution channel to consume up to 10% of
           trailing 90-day organic MINI volume. That is approximately 30% of an
           average month's volume, before other caps. It is too permissive for a
           newly forming market.

  F6-11 — D-0073's 1% of epoch capacity per-human contribution cap allows only
           100 colluding humans to fill an entire contribution epoch. It is too
           concentrated for a mechanism whose stated purpose is preventing
           capital from becoming privileged access.

  F6-12 — D-0073's >=3 sources across >=2 control domains is too small an oracle
           independence floor for a minting mechanism. Two control domains are
           still a dangerously narrow external dependency.

This report resolves all twelve.

  SPEC-00 Constitution.

  * one verified human has one equal vote;
  * no controlling founder/insider allocation;

  * activity rewards as the near-term earning mechanism;
  * concave rewards;

  * robust/median price-oracle intent;
  * fixed constitutional bounds.

  * first-year Human Share release <= approximately 0.1%;
  * bulk release over centuries;

  * service/security <=0.75%;
  * treasury contribution <=0.25%;

  * no founder/investor multiplier;
  * no confiscatory wealth equalization.

  KEEP D-0074'S PERPETUAL EQUAL HUMAN SHARE.
  KEEP ITS 2% / 0.75% / 0.25% / 3% ENVELOPE.
  REPLACE ITS 365-DAY FULL HUMAN VESTING WITH THE MILLENNIUM/BACK-LOADED

  future-generation entry;

2. OUTSIDE RESEARCH THAT CHANGES THE DESIGN
======================================================================

2.1 Sybil economics

Douceur's foundational Sybil result establishes the relevant limit:
without some external scarcity/trust/resource assumption, a distributed system
cannot make unlimited pseudonymous identities disappear by clever accounting.

Tokenomics therefore must NOT pretend that:
  a concave reward curve by itself proves humanity.

Economic defenses are secondary:
  they reduce extraction after File 4 has made identities human-scarce.

This report therefore binds inflationary service and contribution caps to
MatureHuman credentials where per-human caps are security-relevant, while direct
market payments remain permissionless.

2.2 Concave resource allocation

Kelly, Maulloo and Tan's proportional-fairness work is one important formal
precedent for concave utility in network resource allocation: concavity makes
marginal value decrease instead of paying linearly for ever-larger resource
ownership.

Mininet's existing Deep Dive independently reached the same direction:
  R(x)=x^a, 0<a<1.

This report fixes:
  a=0.40 at launch.

The exact exponent is not claimed to be a universal economic constant.
It is selected because it lands in the project's own 0.3–0.5 range and produces
a strong, measurable anti-concentration effect while still rewarding larger
verified service.

2.3 Oracle manipulation

USENIX Security research on DeFi price oracles documents two important facts:

  * short-window price observations are manipulable;
  * even TWAPs are not magic — long windows trade manipulation resistance for
    staleness and can still be affected under adverse market/consensus
    conditions.

The same literature notes robust aggregation such as median-of-sources as a
practical countermeasure, but source independence remains critical.

Mininet's contribution mint is more dangerous than an ordinary display price:
a bad rate creates new native value.

Therefore this report uses:
  multiple independent control domains;
  median across domains;
  long and short windows together;
  conservative min/max direction;
  a fixed haircut;
  hard anomaly pauses;
  market-depth/volume caps.

2.4 Treasury concentration

Empirical work on major DAO treasuries found very high native-asset
concentration and associated market-volatility risk. The exact Mininet asset
mix is different, but the lesson is directly applicable:
  do not let the commons become economically dependent on one asset, issuer, or
  market venue.

The reserve target is therefore changed from 50/40/10 to 45/45/10 across the
two strategic reserve assets plus a diversified contingency sleeve.

This is not a claim that XRP and XMR have equal volatility distributions.
It is a conservative minimax starting point in the absence of a trustworthy
century-scale covariance forecast.

2.5 Unlock cliffs

Recent empirical token-unlock studies find that large predictable unlock events
are associated with adverse price pressure; preliminary 2026 research continues
to find that unlock effects are material, particularly for young markets.

That is supporting evidence, not the constitutional reason for slow vesting.

The constitutional reason is:
  future-generation fairness + Sybil-extraction safety.

The market-microstructure evidence reinforces one implementation choice:
  no giant contribution cliff;
  use a 90-day initial lock followed by smooth release to day 365.

2.6 Vote buying

Juels, Catalano and Jakobsson formalize coercion resistance around an adversary
who may demand a particular vote or proof of compliance.

This matters because Mininet's strongest anti-whale property is formal:
  MINI cannot increase vote weight.

That still does not stop a rich human from buying/coercing other humans
off-network.

The correct long-term answer is not wealth confiscation.
It is secret/receipt-resistant governance where feasible, so a buyer cannot
reliably verify the purchased vote.

This remains a governance-cryptography requirement, not a token-balance rule.

3. QUANTITATIVE CALIBRATION RESULTS
======================================================================

3.1 Passive proportional dilution

If gross protocol issuance is fully used and an early holder receives none of
the new issuance, their fraction of the growing issued-claim supply is multiplied
by:

  1 / (1+r)^Y

At r=3%:

  year 25:   47.7606% of the original proportional position remains
  year 50:   22.8107%
  year 100:   5.2033%
  year 200:   0.2707%

At r=2%:

  year 25:   60.9531%
  year 50:   37.1528%
  year 100:  13.8033%
  year 200:   1.9053%

This is dilution of proportional economic claim.

It is NOT:
  confiscation;
  a promise about market price;
  a promise about liquid circulating supply;
  a guarantee that a whale does not voluntarily buy new MINI.

The 3% ceiling is therefore economically meaningful:
it is large enough to keep future participation economically relevant over
centuries, but bounded enough not to be an unbounded discretionary inflation
lever.

3.2 Why the annual budget must be fixed at the year boundary

If "3% annual" is implemented as 3%/12 of a newly enlarged opening supply every
month:

  (1 + 0.03/12)^12 - 1
  = 0.0304159569
  = 3.04159569%

Overshoot:
  approximately 4.1596 basis points.

With daily rebasing:

  (1 + 0.03/365)^365 - 1
  = 3.04532636%

Overshoot:
  approximately 4.5326 basis points.

This is small in one year but unacceptable as a constitutional correctness
error.

The fix is not a smaller rate.

The fix is:
  freeze the year's budget base once;
  debit all epochs from that fixed budget.

3.3 Reserve shock minimax

D-0073:
  XRP 50%
  XMR 40%
  contingency 10%

One-asset -80% shock:

  XRP shock:
    -40% total reserve

  XMR shock:
    -32% total reserve

Proposed:
  XRP 45%
  XMR 45%
  contingency 10%

One-asset -80% shock:

  XRP shock:
    -36%

  XMR shock:
    -36%

Both assets -80%:
  -72% under either allocation.

The proposal therefore reduces the worst of the two named single-asset shock
losses from 40% to 36% without increasing the simultaneous two-asset shock loss.

3.4 Concave service curve

Launch exponent:
  a=0.40

Effective verified work:
  q_eff = min(q, 10*m)

where:
  q = the human's verified useful work in the service class/epoch;
  m = median positive verified useful work among eligible MatureHuman providers
      in the reference set.

Weight:
  w = (q_eff / m)^0.40 * diversity_multiplier

Before diversity bonus:

  q/m = 0.10 -> 0.3981x
  q/m = 0.25 -> 0.5743x
  q/m = 0.50 -> 0.7579x
  q/m = 1.00 -> 1.0000x
  q/m = 2.00 -> 1.3195x
  q/m = 4.00 -> 1.7411x
  q/m =10.00 -> 2.5119x
  q/m =100.0 -> 2.5119x because the input is capped at10m

Diversity multiplier:
  [1.00, 1.20]

Absolute maximum curve weight relative to a median provider:
  2.5119 * 1.20
  = 3.0143x.

That is a materially different architecture from linear bytes-for-points.

3.5 Absolute service capture

One MatureHuman may receive at most:
  2% of any service sub-pool per economic epoch.

Because the same 2% cap applies independently and the service sub-pools sum to
the full service channel, one human's aggregate inflationary service reward
cannot exceed:

  2% * 0.75%
  = 0.015% of the annual issuance reference base per year.

At least 50 distinct MatureHumans are therefore needed to exhaust any service
sub-pool solely at the per-human caps.

Unused service capacity expires.

This deliberately sacrifices the ability to pay one giant warehouse the whole
subsidy.

That is not a bug.
It is the anti-centralization cost.

The warehouse may still earn:
  direct user fees;
  voluntary payments;
  market revenue.

It cannot monopolize common inflation.

3.6 Absolute contribution capture

Per-MatureHuman contribution cap:
  0.25% of each contribution epoch's mint capacity.

At least:
  400 distinct MatureHumans

are needed to fill a contribution epoch solely at individual caps.

Even if one human hits the cap in all twelve epochs, their maximum protocol
contribution-channel issuance is:

  0.25% * 0.25%
  = 0.000625% of annual reference supply

before the tighter volume/depth/reserve constraints.

Sixty-four colluding humans can consume at most:
  16% of an epoch.

3.7 One extremely wealthy human, protocol-created issuance only

At 2,048 MatureHumans, if one already-rich person receives:

  their equal Human Share;
  the maximum 2% share of every service sub-pool;
  the maximum 0.25% share of every contribution epoch;

their maximum annual protocol-created claim increment, before vesting and
before optional-channel under-utilization, is approximately:

  Human Share:
    2% / 2048 = 0.0009765625% of annual base

  Service:
    0.015%

  Contribution:
    0.000625%

  Total:
    approximately 0.0166016% of annual base.

This does not limit what they may acquire voluntarily in markets.

It does show that the protocol itself does not keep refilling a whale's
dominance.

A simplified full-3%-issuance model in which such a whale captures this maximum
every year, no other market purchases occur, and population stays at 2,048 gives
the following claim shares:

  Initial 10% whale:
    y25  5.07%
    y50  2.71%
    y100 1.04%
    y200 0.58%

  Initial 30% whale:
    y25 14.62%
    y50  7.27%
    y100 2.09%
    y200 0.63%

  Initial 60% whale:
    y25 28.95%
    y50 14.11%
    y100 3.65%
    y200 0.71%

  Initial 90% whale:
    y25 43.27%
    y50 20.96%
    y100 5.21%
    y200 0.80%

This is not a market forecast.

It is a mechanism check:
the protocol-created channels do not preserve an early whale automatically.

3.8 Personhood / Human Share extraction coupling

With a 2% annual Human Share pool and 2,048 MatureHumans, one annual-equivalent
Human Share grant is:

  0.02 / 2048
  = 0.0009765625% of annual reference supply.

If at most 0.1% of that grant is transferable after its first vesting year:

  first-year transferable amount
  <= 0.0000009765625% of annual reference supply.

File 4 additionally limits how quickly a closed set of MatureHuman colluders can
sponsor new MatureHumans.

Therefore early Sybil extraction is controlled by BOTH:
  personhood scarcity
and:
  value release scarcity.

Tokenomics never treats one as a substitute for the other.

4. FINAL MONETARY POLICY — MONETARY POLICY V1
======================================================================

E6-01 — 3.00% IS THE HARD GROSS ANNUAL ISSUANCE CEILING.

A Mininet Economic Year is:

  ECONOMIC_YEAR_MS = 31,536,000,000
                   = 365 days.

Economic year zero begins at:
  mainnet genesis time.

Year N starts at:
  genesis_time + N * ECONOMIC_YEAR_MS.

No calendar authority is needed.

E6-02 — TWELVE EQUAL ECONOMIC EPOCHS PER YEAR.

  ECONOMIC_EPOCH_MS = ECONOMIC_YEAR_MS / 12
                    = 2,628,000,000 ms
                    = approximately 30.4166667 days.

There are exactly twelve.

This avoids:
  variable calendar months;
  leap-year dependence;
  operator discretion.

E6-03 — FREEZE ONE ANNUAL ISSUANCE BASE.

At the beginning of each Economic Year:

  AnnualReferenceSupply_y =
      canonical TotalIssuedClaimSupply at the finalized year-boundary block.

`TotalIssuedClaimSupply` includes:
  vested MINI;
  transferable MINI;
  protocol-issued but still vesting grants.

It excludes:
  unissued capacity;
  invalidated/burned claims.

It does NOT decrease because a holder:
  loses a key;
  voluntarily locks tokens;
  puts tokens in escrow;
  moves tokens to treasury;
  stops trading.

This prevents a private actor from shrinking the issuance denominator by moving
tokens into a contract.

E6-04 — ZERO-SUPPLY BOOTSTRAP FLOOR.

A percentage-of-supply system has a zero-supply singularity.

The current documents do not resolve it cleanly.

Final rule:

  EffectiveAnnualReference_y =
    max(
      AnnualReferenceSupply_y,
      ActiveMatureHumansAtYearOpen * 1 MINI
    ).

`1 MINI per Active MatureHuman` is an ACCOUNTING REFERENCE FLOOR.

It is NOT:
  a premine;
  an owned balance;
  a treasury asset;
  a founder allocation.

Why one MINI:
  with six-decimal uMINI precision, a 2% annual Human Share generated from a
  one-MINI-per-human floor gives 20,000 uMINI of annual Human Share capacity per
  human before division across epochs; this preserves meaningful integer
  precision even at bootstrap without assigning phantom property to anyone.

Once issued-claim supply exceeds the human reference floor:
  ordinary supply-based compounding governs.

E6-05 — ANNUAL CHANNEL BUDGETS ARE FIXED FROM THAT BASE.

  HumanBudget_y
    = floor(EffectiveAnnualReference_y * 0.020000)

  ServiceBudget_y
    = floor(EffectiveAnnualReference_y * 0.007500)

  ContributionBudget_y
    = floor(EffectiveAnnualReference_y * 0.002500)

  GrossBudget_y
    = floor(EffectiveAnnualReference_y * 0.030000)

Every epoch debits these year-fixed ledgers.

No epoch recalculates its allowance from newly issued supply.

E6-06 — CHANNEL CAPACITY IS NON-FUNGIBLE.

Human Share capacity cannot be moved to:
  service;
  contribution;
  treasury;
  founder grant.

Unused service capacity cannot be moved to:
  contribution.

Unused contribution capacity cannot be moved to:
  service.

Unused optional capacity:
  expires.

Human rounding remainder:
  remains unissued.

This prevents a governance majority from starving Human Share and reclassifying
the budget as its preferred program.

E6-07 — HUMAN SHARE IS THE PROTECTED PRIORITY.

Within each epoch:

  human capacity is computed first.

Optional service/contribution plans are valid only after reserving that epoch's
Human Share budget.

If arithmetic or total-cap reconciliation fails:
  optional issuance fails;
  Human Share is not cut to make room.

E6-08 — NO EMERGENCY MINT.

No:
  bridge crisis;
  treasury loss;
  exchange delisting;
  developer payroll;
  lawsuit;
  security incident;
  validator vote;
  founder request;
  "systemically important" institution

can exceed the annual budgets.

A fork may change the constitution.
The conforming network must reject out-of-budget state.

E6-09 — BURNING DOES NOT CREATE NEW ISSUANCE CAPACITY IN THE SAME YEAR.

Burn:
  reduces spendable/claim supply according to normal ownership rules.

It does not replenish:
  the current year's already-fixed issuance budget.

At the next year boundary:
  the canonical issued claim supply reflects prior burns.

This removes burn-and-remint games.

5. FINAL HUMAN SHARE MODEL
======================================================================

E6-10 — HUMAN SHARE REMAINS A PERPETUAL EQUAL STREAM.

There is no finite first-generation bag.

Every economic epoch:
  the Human Share budget slice is divided equally among the File 4
  Active MatureHuman snapshot for that epoch.

No input includes:
  balance;
  wealth;
  country;
  age after eligibility;
  hardware capacity;
  storage;
  reputation;
  contribution;
  employment;
  founder status;
  purchase history;
  vote.

E6-11 — PRE-MATURE HUMAN SHARE IS ZERO.

File 4 statuses below Active MatureHuman:
  receive no transferable Human Share;
  receive no canonical Human Share grant.

This is stricter and simpler than accumulating large speculative provisional
claims for unverified identities.

E6-12 — EQUAL-EPOCH FORMULA.

For epoch e in year y:

  HumanCapacity_e =
    remaining HumanBudget_y constrained by the exact prorated epoch slice.

For the twelve equal epochs:

  nominal_slice =
    floor(HumanBudget_y / 12)

with deterministic distribution of at most eleven uMINI of annual division
remainder to no one:
  remainder remains unissued.

Then:

  PerHumanGrant_e =
    floor(HumanCapacity_e / EligibleMatureHumans_e)

  IssuedHuman_e =
    PerHumanGrant_e * EligibleMatureHumans_e

  HumanRemainder_e =
    HumanCapacity_e - IssuedHuman_e

HumanRemainder_e:
  remains unissued;
  is never awarded by lottery;
  is never redirected.

E6-13 — HUMAN SHARE VESTING IS BACK-LOADED ACROSS ONE THOUSAND YEARS.

Each epoch's Human Share grant has its own `active_vesting_age`.

Cumulative vested fraction V(a), where a is ACTIVE MATURE years since grant:

  0 <= a <= 1:

    V(a) = 0.001 * a

  1 < a <= 10:

    V(a) =
      0.001 + 0.199 * (a - 1) / 9

  10 < a <= 1000:

    V(a) =
      0.20 + 0.80 * (a - 10) / 990

  a >= 1000:

    V(a) = 1.00

Reference points:

  active year   1 ->  0.1%
  active year   5 ->  8.9444%
  active year  10 -> 20.0%
  active year  25 -> 21.2121%
  active year  50 -> 23.2323%
  active year 100 -> 27.2727%
  active year 200 -> 35.3535%
  active year 500 -> 59.5960%
  active year1000 ->100.0%

This deliberately creates:
  tiny first-year extraction;
  meaningful decade-scale participation;
  very long residual release.

E6-14 — `active_vesting_age`, NOT WALL CLOCK, DRIVES HUMAN VESTING.

A grant's vesting age advances only while its owner is:
  Active MatureHuman under File 4.

During File 4 grace/dormancy:
  no new Human Share grant;
  existing unvested grant is frozen;
  already vested MINI remains property.

If the human reactivates:
  vesting age resumes prospectively.

No catch-up for inactive time.

No confiscation occurs merely because:
  illness;
  imprisonment;
  displacement;
  censorship;
  device loss;
  political speech;
  moderation dispute;
  inactivity.

E6-15 — NO CONDUCT-BASED HUMAN SHARE CLAWBACK.

The older SPEC-05 language allowing "Sybil clawback" cannot be implemented as a
human/jury/admin decision.

Under File 4's no-de-personing rule:

  vested MINI is never clawed back for conduct.

An unvested duplicate credential's grants may be invalidated only if:
  File 4's approved deterministic cryptographic duplicate rule proves that the
  credential was not an independent MatureHuman credential.

No accusation:
  burns money.

E6-16 — DEATH / PERMANENT ABSENCE DOES NOT CREATE AN ADMIN ESTATE KEY.

If presence never resumes:
  unvested Human Share stays locked and its vesting age does not advance.

Vested transferable property:
  follows holder-controlled inheritance/key arrangements.

No central administrator:
  decides death;
  inherits the unvested stream;
  recycles the balance by discretion.

Long-term state compaction may replace old permanently inactive grant objects
with commitments, but must not change ownership semantics.

E6-17 — HUMAN SHARE CANNOT BE SOLD BEFORE VESTING.

Unvested grant:
  non-transferable;
  non-collateralizable at protocol level;
  non-delegable;
  no governance power;
  no validator power.

The protocol cannot stop an off-chain human from signing a private contract
about future value.

It does not provide a native enforcement mechanism for such contracts.

6. FINAL SERVICE / SECURITY REWARD MODEL
======================================================================

E6-18 — SERVICE/SECURITY INFLATION IS CAPPED AT 0.75% PER YEAR.

It is a ceiling, not a promise.

Unused:
  expires.

Fees paid voluntarily/directly by users are transfers of existing MINI and are
not part of the 0.75% issuance cap.

E6-19 — LAUNCH SERVICE SUB-POOLS.

The 0.75% annual service budget is initially partitioned:

  40%  Storage / availability
  20%  Relay / routing
  15%  Publisher / content-serving
  10%  Search / indexing
  15%  Security / objective public-good proofs

Equivalent maximum annual reference-supply issuance:

  Storage:
    0.3000%

  Relay:
    0.1500%

  Publisher/content:
    0.1125%

  Search:
    0.0750%

  Security:
    0.1125%

Subjective work such as:
  code quality judgment;
  translation quality;
  moderation judgment;
  legal work;
  audit opinion

is NOT auto-minted from an algorithmic service score.

It is paid through:
  transparent bounties;
  treasury proposals;
  direct voluntary payments.

This prevents an automated "reputation oracle" from becoming a patronage
authority.

E6-20 — SUB-POOL SHARES MAY BE TUNED ONLY WITHIN BOUNDS.

One-human-one-vote + timelock may tune once per Economic Year within:

  Storage:    30%..50% of service budget
  Relay:      10%..25%
  Publisher:  10%..25%
  Search:      5%..15%
  Security:   10%..25%

All must sum to 100%.

No class:
  >50%.

A parameter change activates only at the next Economic Year.

E6-21 — INFLATIONARY SERVICE REWARD REQUIRES MATURE HUMAN CAP BINDING.

Any node may:
  store;
  route;
  publish;
  search;
  contribute service;
  receive ordinary user fees.

But common inflationary subsidy needs a File 4 MatureHuman nullifier/claim
binding so one human cannot multiply the per-human cap through many devices.

The service proof must not publicly expose the person's ordinary identities.

E6-22 — VERIFIED USEFUL WORK, NOT PURCHASED CAPACITY, IS THE INPUT.

For each service class:
  q is successfully verified useful service over the epoch.

Examples:

  storage:
    valid independent storage/serve/proof units, not raw disk owned;

  routing:
    authenticated useful forwarded traffic under privacy-safe receipt design,
    not self-reported bandwidth;

  content-serving:
    independently requested/served content units with anti-self-dealing rules;

  search:
    verifiable query/service availability units where privacy allows;

  security:
    objective protocol-verifiable actions/proofs, not subjective "goodness."

A 100 TB idle disk does not earn merely because it exists.

E6-23 — CONCAVE CURVE.

For a service class and epoch:

  m = median positive verified work among eligible MatureHuman providers.

If no eligible positive providers:
  issue zero.

For provider h:

  r_h = q_h / m

  r_cap = min(r_h, 10)

  base_weight_h = r_cap ^ 0.40

Use a deterministic fixed-point approximation/table proven monotonic and
concave to implementation precision.

Floating point is not consensus arithmetic.

E6-24 — DIVERSITY BONUS IS SMALL AND BOUNDED.

  diversity_multiplier in [1.00, 1.20].

It may reward demonstrably under-represented:
  network domains;
  geographic coarse regions;
  failure domains.

It must not require:
  raw GPS;
  government address;
  ISP identity disclosure;
  paid attestation.

Any diversity predicate must be privacy-preserving and independently
verifiable enough for the security value it claims.

If robust diversity evidence is unavailable:
  multiplier = 1.00.

Never guess.

E6-25 — 2% PER-HUMAN SUB-POOL CAP.

After weights are computed and normalized:

  Reward_h,class <= 0.02 * SubPool_e,class.

If cap causes unallocated remainder:
  redistribute among uncapped eligible providers using the same weights,
  iteratively.

If every remaining provider is capped:
  leftover expires.

No provider can get around the cap with:
  more devices;
  more wallets;
  more storage identities.

E6-26 — CONCAVITY IS NOT GOVERNANCE WEIGHT.

Storage/service work may affect:
  value reward;
  separately governed selection probability where SPEC-05 allows.

It never changes:
  finality vote weight;
  governance vote weight;
  personhood score merely because wealth bought hardware.

7. FINAL TREASURY CONTRIBUTION MODEL
======================================================================

E6-27 — ORDINARY BRIDGE TRADING AND CONTRIBUTION MINTING ARE DIFFERENT.

Ordinary bridge:
  locks existing canonical MINI;
  creates/redeems a 1:1 external twin according to bridge rules;
  does not create new MINI.

Treasury contribution:
  accepted external reserve asset enters commons reserve;
  bounded native MINI is newly issued from the 0.25% channel.

Code and UI must never merge these.

E6-28 — CONTRIBUTION CHANNEL IS DISABLED AT MAINNET GENESIS UNTIL MARKET
READINESS.

This supersedes older "live from block 1" language.

Reason:
  a contribution mint denominated by market price is circular before MINI has a
  credible market price.

Ordinary native MINI:
  can exist;
  transfer;
  earn;
  trade.

Treasury contribution mint activates only after Section 11's market-readiness
criteria pass.

E6-29 — CONTRIBUTION ISSUANCE CAP REMAINS 0.25% OF ANNUAL REFERENCE SUPPLY.

No:
  reserve shortage;
  price opportunity;
  whale offer;
  governance vote
can exceed it.

E6-30 — EACH CONTRIBUTION EPOCH GETS 1/12 OF THE FIXED ANNUAL CAP.

  EpochContributionBudget =
    floor(ContributionBudget_y / 12)

Unused:
  expires at epoch end.

No rollover.

Year-to-date debit prevents:
  overshoot from rounding.

E6-31 — ACTUAL EPOCH CAP IS THE MINIMUM OF FOUR LIMITS.

  ContributionCapacity_e =
    min(
      fixed_epoch_contribution_budget,
      remaining_annual_contribution_budget,
      0.02 * Trailing90dOrganicMiniVolume,
      ReserveAcquisitionGapValueInMini
    )

This replaces D-0073's:
  10% of trailing-90-day volume

with:
  2%.

`OrganicMiniVolume` excludes:
  self-trades proven by protocol data where detectable;
  contribution issuance itself;
  bridge mint/burn bookkeeping;
  treasury internal rebalance;
  wash-liquidity generated by the same economic actor where objectively
  linkable.

The system must not pretend all wash trading is detectable.
Market-depth criteria are a second brake.

E6-32 — PER-MATURE-HUMAN CONTRIBUTION CAP IS 0.25% OF EPOCH CAPACITY.

A person proves:
  MatureHuman membership
and:
  one contribution-scope nullifier.

The proof does not add governance power.

Multiple wallets:
  share the same human cap.

E6-33 — OVERSUBSCRIPTION IS PRO RATA, NOT FIRST-COME-FIRST-SERVED.

If valid demand D > ContributionCapacity_e:

  each accepted contribution receives:

    accepted_i =
      floor(requested_i * Capacity / D)

subject to per-human cap.

Deterministic remainder:
  remains unissued/unaccepted rather than awarded by ordering lottery.

Excess external funds:
  must not be taken.

Protocol either:
  accepts only the computed amount;
  or has a trustless/refundable staging design.

It must not accept money first and decide later that no MINI is available.

E6-34 — CONTRIBUTION RATE HAS A 10% RESERVE-PROTECTION HAIRCUT.

After robust external-asset and MINI prices are calculated:

  raw_rate = conservative_external_price / conservative_MINI_price

  contribution_rate = raw_rate * 0.90

Contributor gets:
  90% of the contemporaneous conservative market-equivalent MINI.

Commons receives:
  100% of accepted reserve asset.

This 10% is:
  not an operator fee;
  not paid to signers;
  not discretionary income;
  a reserve-protection margin.

Governance may increase the haircut up to 20% with ordinary timelocked
parameter process.

It may not reduce below 10% without reopening this calibrated economic scope.

E6-35 — CONTRIBUTION VESTING IS ONE YEAR WITH NO 90-DAY CLIFF DUMP.

Final contribution vesting:

  age < 90 days:
    0% transferable

  90 <= age <365 days:
    vested fraction =
      (age_days - 90) / 275

  age >=365:
    100%

This means:
  no contribution-issued MINI is transferable for the first 90 days;
  after day90 it unlocks smoothly;
  no single day90 cliff releases the whole amount.

E6-36 — CONTRIBUTION CLAIM IS NOT GOVERNANCE COLLATERAL.

Unvested contribution MINI:
  no governance power.

Vested MINI:
  still no governance power because all MINI has zero vote weight.

E6-37 — CONTRIBUTION CANNOT BYPASS RECEIPT FINALITY.

Economic capacity/rate approval is not proof external funds arrived.

Mint requires:
  chain-specific cryptographic/finality receipt mechanism approved elsewhere.

If receipt verification is unavailable:
  mint = disabled.

No trusted operator boolean:
  substitutes merely because the rate is economically safe.

E6-38 — LAUNCH TARGET:

  XRP / XRPL-native liquid exposure: 45%
  XMR:                              45%
  Contingency:                      10%

The exact asset implementation remains subject to the legal/custody decisions

E6-39 — HARD ACQUISITION CEILINGS.

Normal acquisition target bands:

  XRP:
    target 45%
    acquisition allowed while post-acquisition weight <=50%

  XMR:
    target 45%
    acquisition allowed while post-acquisition weight <=50%

  contingency:
    target 10%
    acquisition allowed while total <=10%

A market move can push a held asset above 50%.

That does NOT trigger forced selling.

Instead:
  stop new acquisition of the overweight asset;
  direct new acceptable contributions/rebalancing toward underweight assets.

This avoids a predictable forced-liquidation mechanism.

E6-40 — CONTINGENCY IS DIVERSIFIED BY CONTROL DOMAIN.

No single issuer/custodian/control domain:
  >5% of total reserve through the contingency sleeve.

No two contingency instruments under one parent:
  count as two domains.

If only one suitable contingency asset exists:
  hold at most5%;
  the remaining contingency target stays unfilled or sits in a non-issuer
  self-custodied asset permitted by governance.

Do not create central dependency just to reach 10%.

E6-41 — RESERVE VALUE IS INFORMATION, NOT A REDEMPTION PROMISE.

No holder has a protocol promise:
  1 MINI redeemable for proportional XRP/XMR reserve.

The reserve:
  strengthens the commons;
  funds approved common actions;
  provides resilience.

It is not:
  a currency board;
  a stablecoin guarantee.

A run on a nonexistent redemption promise must not become a protocol failure.

E6-42 — BTC IS ZERO-WEIGHT AT LAUNCH UNLESS THE SEPARATE ADOPTED LEGAL/BRIDGE
DECISION CHANGES IT.

Historical specs remain historical.

E6-43 — RESERVE PRICE DOES NOT CONTROL GOVERNANCE.

A collapse of:
  XRP;
  XMR;
  contingency issuer

can destroy reserve purchasing power.

It cannot:
  change votes;
  change personhood;
  change finality weights;
  change constitutional validity;
  halt native Human Share calculation.

The reserve is an edge asset pool, not Mininet's root of legitimacy.

9. FINAL PRICE-ORACLE MODEL FOR CONTRIBUTION MINTING
======================================================================

E6-44 — ROSTER FLOOR: SEVEN INDEPENDENT CONTROL DOMAINS.

Production contribution pricing maintains a source roster of at least:

  7 independent control domains.

A control domain means:
  one operator/parent organization or economically controlled venue family.

Five brands owned by one parent:
  one domain.

A CEX and its own API mirrors:
  one domain.

E6-45 — QUORUM: FIVE INDEPENDENT DOMAINS.

A price bucket is usable only if:
  >=5 independent control domains provide valid observations.

Fewer than5:
  contribution pricing pauses.

Native MINI:
  continues.

E6-46 — ONE DOMAIN, ONE WEIGHT.

Do not weight a large exchange more heavily merely because it is large.

Per 5-minute observation bucket:

  collect one canonical price per control domain;
  sort;
  take median.

This resists:
  one giant venue becoming the oracle.

E6-47 — FIVE-MINUTE BUCKETS.

Oracle observations normalize into:

  5-minute UTC-independent chain-time buckets derived from finalized block time.

Missing observations:
  are missing;
  never forward-filled indefinitely.

E6-48 — TWO WINDOWS.

For each priced asset and MINI:

  P24 =
    median of valid five-minute bucket medians from the preceding24h.

  P30 =
    median of valid five-minute bucket medians from the preceding30d.

Use equal bucket weight.

This is a time-weighted median-like construction.

E6-49 — CONSERVATIVE DIRECTION.

For incoming external asset A:

  ConservativePrice_A =
    min(P24_A, P30_A)

For MINI:

  ConservativePrice_MINI =
    max(P24_MINI, P30_MINI)

Then:

  raw_rate =
    ConservativePrice_A / ConservativePrice_MINI.

This always selects the conservative direction for newly issued MINI:
  lower valuation for what the commons receives;
  higher valuation for what it issues.

Then apply:
  10% haircut.

E6-50 — VOLATILITY / DISLOCATION PAUSE.

For either A or MINI:

  if P24/P30 <0.75
or:
  P24/P30 >1.25

then:
  new contribution acceptance pauses.

Existing vested property:
  unaffected.

Reason:
  a 25% short/long divergence is a market-regime change, not a condition in
  which a minting oracle should pretend precision.

E6-51 — ROBUST CROSS-DOMAIN DISPERSION TEST.

For each valid five-minute bucket with prices sorted:

  x1 <= x2 <= ... <= xn

For n>=5:

  central_span =
    (x_{n-1} - x_2) / median

using the second-lowest through second-highest range.

If central_span >10% in more than5% of required buckets in the current24h:
  contribution pauses.

Using the central span rather than min/max means:
  one malicious extreme source cannot single-handedly create a denial of
  service.

E6-52 — COVERAGE FLOOR.

Both 24h and30d windows need:
  >=95% of expected five-minute buckets usable.

For new markets during the initial 90-day bootstrap:
  contribution remains disabled anyway under Section11.

E6-53 — SOURCE ROSTER CHANGES ARE PROSPECTIVE AND TIMELOCKED.

Minimum timelock:
  30 days.

New roster activates:
  at the next Economic Epoch after the timelock.

It cannot:
  reprice old contributions;
  rewrite historic buckets.

E6-54 — ORACLE FAILURE IS LOCALIZED.

Oracle failure may stop:
  contribution mint;
  fiat/reference fee retuning.

It must not stop:
  native transfers;
  governance voting;
  personhood;
  finality;
  Human Share accounting from canonical supply/personhood state.

E6-55 — NO SINGLE "OFFICIAL PRICE."

Wallets and markets may display:
  any price source.

The contribution oracle is:
  a bounded mint-safety mechanism;
  not a claim that governance knows MINI's true economic value.

  the exact oracle-window commitment;
  haircut;
  capacity;

rule.

No privileged negotiated rate.

E6-60 — FRONT-RUNNING DOES NOT CHANGE ALLOCATION.

Oversubscription:
  pro rata.

E6-61 — NO VIP / OTC PROTOCOL RATE.

Governance cannot give:

  country;

E6-62 — CONTRIBUTOR PRIVACY.

The economic rule needs:
  one-MatureHuman cap proof
without:
  public legal identity;
  persistent cross-epoch contributor identity.

Use File4-style scoped nullifier mechanics.

The incoming chain transaction may still reveal chain-native information.
Do not add unnecessary Mininet identity linkage.

12. FEE ACCESS / WEALTH-GATE DECISION

The fee mechanism exists to ration scarce compute/storage/chain inclusion.

It must not become:
  a poll tax on political humanity.

E6-63 — VOICE-CRITICAL ACTIONS HAVE ZERO MONETARY ACCESS FEE.

The protocol may not require a MINI balance merely to:

  cast one valid governance vote;
  maintain/refresh baseline personhood;
  submit the ordinary proof required to exercise one-human-one-vote;
  perform a constitutional challenge/fraud proof where the challenger would
  otherwise be excluded solely for poverty.

These paths use:
  nullifiers;
  per-human limits;
  bounded proof sizes;
  ordinary network anti-DoS mechanisms.

An optional transaction relay may charge for convenience.
A conforming path with zero monetary fee must exist.

E6-64 — VALUE TRANSFERS MAY HAVE A SMALL MARKET-ADJUSTED FEE.

Launch reference target for an ordinary native value transfer:

  0.001 of a 2026 USD-equivalent unit
  = one tenth of one cent.

Protocol safety ceiling for an ordinary non-congested native transfer target:

  0.01 USD-equivalent
  = one cent.

This is an affordability calibration, not a currency peg.

The reference may be replaced by a broader purchasing-power basket under
ordinary governance if a century-scale implementation proves preferable.

E6-65 — FEE ORACLE FAILURE MAY NOT HALT THE CHAIN.

Fee target is recalculated only from a valid robust oracle observation.

If current external reference pricing fails:
  keep the last valid bounded fee schedule for up to30 days.

After30 days:
  use a deterministic low fixed uMINI fallback established in the prior valid
  epoch.

No external fiat-price provider:
  can stop transfers or votes.

E6-66 — CONGESTION PRICING CANNOT CHANGE VOTE WEIGHT.

If blocks are congested:
  value-transfer fees may rise inside separately specified caps.

Governance vote inclusion:
  retains the zero-money access lane and per-human rate limit.

A wealthy user may buy more value-transaction throughput.

They may not buy:
  more votes.

13. WHALE RESISTANCE — WHAT IS AND IS NOT SOLVED
======================================================================

E6-67 — NO HOLDINGS CAP.

Mininet does NOT:
  confiscate;
  periodically equalize balances;
  tax a person solely for being rich;
  forbid voluntary market purchases;
  cap how much MINI a human may own.

Such rules would require:
  surveillance;
  identity linkage;
  central discretionary enforcement
or:
  make free exchange fictional.

They are rejected.

E6-68 — FORMAL POWER IS ABSOLUTELY SEPARATED.

The following functions/interfaces must have no positive dependency on MINI
balance:

  governance vote weight;
  proposal tally weight;
  personhood score;
  vouch capacity;
  MatureHuman status;
  validator BFT vote weight;
  constitutional validity;
  release-approval weight;
  maintainer review authority;
  content moderation authority;
  treasury signer eligibility;
  oracle observation weight.

Formal political influence of a whale:
  one MatureHuman vote.

Formal political influence of a poor MatureHuman:
  one MatureHuman vote.

E6-69 — ECONOMIC INFLUENCE IS REAL AND NOT MISLABELED.

A rich holder can still:
  buy services;
  fund content;
  hire people;
  fund campaigns;
  subsidize forks;
  pay real humans;
  offer bribes;
  move a thin market.

Tokenomics cannot mathematically erase those social facts without becoming
authoritarian itself.

The design response is:
  no formal balance power;
  secret/receipt-resistant ballots;
  reach floors;
  per-human common-issuance caps;
  diversified market/liquidity paths;
  free forks;
  transparent treasury decisions.

E6-70 — NO GLOBAL WEALTH-GINI PASS/FAIL TARGET.

A holdings Gini can rise because:
  people voluntarily trade;
  one artist sells valuable work;
  one company earns legitimate market revenue.

That does not automatically violate Mininet.

The protocol is judged on:
  distribution of protocol-created issuance;
  access to voice;
  concentration of common subsidy;
  absence of balance-weighted power.

The simulation still reports Gini/top shares as diagnostics.

It does not confiscate to hit a number.

E6-71 — PROTOCOL-CREATED CONCENTRATION HAS HARD TARGETS.

Human Share:
  exactly equal per eligible MatureHuman per epoch.

Inflationary service:
  <=2% of each class pool per MatureHuman.

Contribution channel:
  <=0.25% of epoch capacity per MatureHuman.

Founder:
  no multiplier.

Early adopter:
  no multiplier on future Human Share.

E6-72 — SECRET / RECEIPT-RESISTANT GOVERNANCE REMAINS A HARDENING REQUIREMENT.

If vote protocol produces a transferable receipt that proves how a person voted:
  vote buying becomes easier.

The long-term governance proof should minimize verifiable vote receipts and
coercion evidence while preserving public tally correctness.

This is not necessary to define monetary supply.

It is necessary to claim strong off-network whale resistance.

14. SUPPLY ACCOUNTING / ARITHMETIC
======================================================================

E6-73 — DISTINGUISH FOUR QUANTITIES.

Consensus accounting exposes:

  TotalIssuedClaimSupply
    all valid native MINI claims ever issued minus protocol burns.

  VestedSupply
    portion whose protocol vesting condition has completed.

  TransferableSupply
    vested units not under a protocol-level transfer lock;
    user escrow does NOT reduce the annual reference base.

  UnissuedCapacity
    budget permission that has not produced a claim.
    This is NOT supply.

AnnualReferenceSupply uses:
  TotalIssuedClaimSupply.

Market dashboards may display:
  circulating/transferable estimates.

Monetary consensus must never depend on an exchange's "circulating supply"
website.

E6-74 — U128 IS THE NATIVE MONETARY INTEGER WIDTH.

Current `Amount` uses u128 micro-units.

Keep it.

All:
  multiplication;
  division;
  prorating;
  price conversion;
  reward normalization
must use checked arithmetic or reduction-before-multiplication.

No monetary `as u64` narrowing.

E6-75 — FAIL ON OVERFLOW.

`mint_amount_micro` becomes conceptually:

  fn mint_amount(
      contributed_units: u128,
      rate_num: u128,
      rate_den: u128
  ) -> Result<Amount>

Use:
  gcd reduction;
  checked_mul;
  checked_div;
  explicit zero-denominator rejection.

Overflow:
  transaction invalid.

Never:
  wrap;
  saturate;
  truncate.

E6-76 — 1000-YEAR NUMERIC HEADROOM.

At 3% compound growth:

  (1.03)^1000
  approximately 6.87424e12.

With six-decimal micro units, if the genesis/reference supply is constrained to:

  <=1e18 MINI

then even the impossible worst case of full 3% gross issuance every year for
1000 years remains below u128 maximum with substantial headroom.

Therefore production genesis validation SHALL reject:

  EffectiveAnnualReference_0 > 1e18 MINI.

This is an engineering numeric ceiling, not an economic target.

Realistic supply is expected to be vastly lower.

E6-77 — ROUND DOWN, NEVER MINT THE ROUNDING ERROR.

Every division of issuance capacity:
  floors toward zero.

Remainder:
  remains unissued.

Do not:
  rotate remainder winner;
  send it to treasury;
  credit proposer.

This is deterministic and privilege-free.

E6-78 — PRICE ARITHMETIC IS RATIONAL / FIXED POINT.

No IEEE floating-point consensus arithmetic.

Each oracle price:
  integer numerator/denominator with bounded decimal exponent
or:
  canonical fixed point.

Median compares exact normalized integers.

The contribution rate:
  uses checked rational arithmetic.

E6-79 — STATE COMMITMENTS INCLUDE YEAR-TO-DATE BUDGET USE.

A monetary state root commits at least:

  economic_year;
  EffectiveAnnualReference;
  HumanBudget;
  HumanUsed;
  ServiceBudget;
  ServiceUsed;
  ContributionBudget;
  ContributionUsed;
  active policy version.

A node replaying history can prove:
  the 3% ceiling
without:
  reconstructing market websites.

Reopen this scope if a future change:

  * raises gross annual issuance above3%;
  * lowers Human Share below2% while this monetary model is canonical;
  * raises service above0.75%;
  * raises contribution above0.25%;
  * shortens Human first-year vesting beyond the0.1% ceiling;
  * materially shortens the1000-year Human vest tail;
  * pays non-Mature identities inflationary Human Share;
  * makes service reward linear/convex above the approved bounds;
  * raises service per-human class capture above2%;
  * raises contribution per-human epoch cap above0.25%;
  * lowers contribution haircut below10%;

  * lowers depth multiplier below20;
  * lowers oracle roster below7 independent domains;
  * lowers oracle quorum below5;
  * creates a guaranteed reserve redemption promise;
  * makes one reserve issuer/control domain indispensable;
  * introduces balance-weighted governance;

    issuance denominator;
  * introduces unchecked monetary narrowing/overflow;
  * materially changes contribution pricing to a new oracle security model.

  * market price changes;

DOES THE CURRENT ECONOMIC IMPLEMENTATION ALREADY SERVE A FREE INTERNET FOR
HUMANITY AT REAL-VALUE SCALE?

NO.

Exact reasons:

  * Human Share fully vests in one year in current D-0074 code;
  * no canonical annual budget ledger prevents epoch-rebase overshoot;
  * production policy ratios are caller-supplied rather than protocol-bound;
  * current storage reward is linear before a rate/window cap, not the frozen
    concave mechanism;
  * treasury rate is an arbitrary governed scalar, not a robust independent-
    domain oracle;
  * contribution conversion narrows to u64 without explicit overflow failure;
  * current simulation does not model the mechanisms it is supposed to calibrate.

DOES THE ECONOMIC ARCHITECTURE APPROVED IN THIS REPORT SERVE A FREE INTERNET FOR
HUMANITY?

YES.

Why:

  * common issuance is dominated by equal Human Share, not capital;
  * gross inflation is hard-bounded to3% against a year-fixed base;
  * 2% is protected for equal MatureHumans;
  * service inflation is only0.75% and concave/capped;
  * contribution inflation is only0.25% and deeply capacity-limited;
  * a single service human can capture at most2% of any class pool;
  * a single contributor can capture at most0.25% of an epoch;
  * a rich holder can own value but gains zero extra formal voice;
  * Human Share releases <=0.1% of each grant in its first active year and
    keeps a1000-year tail;
  * future humans enter the same current Human Share stream;
  * no confiscatory holdings equalization is needed;
  * reserve risk is diversified without pretending backing is guaranteed;
  * oracle sources are independent and replaceable edges;
  * oracle failure pauses only the optional contribution mechanism;
  * external markets can disappear without stopping native governance/Human Share;
  * no founder, exchange, bank, reserve issuer, auditor, AI, or treasury signer
    has an economic override key.
